About Home Rental

A Platform Where Homeowners & Renters Connect

Home Rental is a full-stack real estate and rental management platform designed to eliminate middleman hassles, verify properties, and make house and room renting transparent, affordable, and fast.

1. Homeowners

List verified apartments, villas, and independent floors with high-resolution photos and pricing. Receive direct tenant enquiries.

2. Rental Managers

Manage single room spaces, co-living setups, and room availability with custom pricing and dimensions.

3. Tenants & Customers

Search homes by budget, locality, and BHK count. View interactive maps, save favourites, and send direct rental enquiries.

4. Administrators

Audit listings, verify quality, approve or reject homes with feedback, and keep the ecosystem safe and trustworthy.
